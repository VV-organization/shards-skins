Source issue https://linear.app/finext-io/issue/VV-163. This approval supersedes the 2026-10-11 spec's guest email-login and no-merge restrictions.

5. Full legal HTML from original DOCX, only 6.3 rate corrected to 1 RUB = 1.6 Shards. Smartvesta footer and Frags SVGs. FAQ href is correct; desktop oversized wordmark hit area overlapped navigation. Raise footer navigation stacking order without changing composition; verify actual click on four widths.
6. Node24 unit, isolated shards_test integration, API contract, typecheck, lint, build, Playwright and visual QA at 1440/768/390/320, fresh final review.

## Constraints
Preserve original designer stylesheet and protected assets/animation; add vv163.css. No secrets, production data or deployment configs from siblings. No real payments, production changes, push or deployment. Rate integer 8/5. Preserve checkout vs fulfillment states and payment retry identity. Guest data inaccessible by email alone. Per-payment matching prevents a shared browser's other-email payments being linked.

## Runtime switch
`GUEST_STEAM_REFILL_ENABLED` defaults to `false`. It must be explicitly enabled together with the existing payment/fulfillment flags and configured providers to expose guest checkout. This work does not enable it or deploy. Existing guest status reads remain authorized by the original cookie when new payments are disabled. Guest cookie lifetime: 30 days; after expiry, past payments are accessible through a Steam cabinet after confirming the payment email. Original legal source documents and extraction provenance: `docs/legal/README.md`.
